~20 yrsIn the HVAC field
JourneymanHVAC license
EPA 608Universal certified
ESCOExam proctor

I've been turning wrenches for about 20 years: service calls, rooftop units, VRF systems and startups. I've lived through the R-22 to R-410A change, and now the move to A2L refrigerants like R-454B is landing on every truck.

I built this course while studying for my own A2L certification. Every lesson started as my study notes, and the wrong answers in the quiz are the mistakes I've actually seen techs make.

I learn by breaking things down until I can explain them to a new helper. If I can't say it simply, I don't know it yet. That's how this course is written.

Why I built it

  • Most A2L training is a few videos and one quiz. Techs need practice, not just a certificate.
  • Techs learn on their phones, in the truck, between calls. Everything here works on a phone.
  • Field knowledge gets lost when people change jobs. I want it written down where the next tech can use it.

For schools and programs

I'm putting together A2L classes for trade programs, job-training groups and community colleges, with practice exams students can take on their own. If you run a program, I'd like to talk.
