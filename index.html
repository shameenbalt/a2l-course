Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The lessons, practice exam, cheat sheet and glossary are free while the course is being built.

Will this certify me?

No. This is exam prep. A2L certification comes from a proctored exam given by a certifying organization. This course helps you walk in ready.

Do I need an account?

No. Your XP, streak and best score are saved on your own phone or computer.

How long does it take?

Each lesson is about 10 minutes. A full practice exam takes 20 to 40 minutes.

Do I still need EPA 608?

Yes. EPA Section 608 still covers A2L work. A2L training is on top of it, not instead of it.
