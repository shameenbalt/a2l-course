Recovery tank fill check

Before you recover into a tank, you need to know where to stop. Read three numbers off the tank and the scale, type them in, and this page tells you the scale reading to stop at.

Where to find the numbers

  • 1WC, water capacity: how much water fills the tank, in pounds.
  • 2TW, tare weight: what the empty tank weighs.
  • 3Service pressure: the number after 4BA or 4BW. Here it's 400 psig.
  • 4Test date: month and year. If you see more than one, use the newest.
  • 5Scale reading: tank and refrigerant together.

    How it works

    1. Refrigerant in the tank = scale reading minus TW.
    2. The plain rule: never more than 80% of WC. WC is in pounds of water, so this rule assumes refrigerant weighs about the same as water.
    3. The hot-day check: R-410A, R-32 and R-454B are lighter than water when they're hot. On a 130°F day, 80% of WC in pounds of R-410A fills about 92% of the tank with liquid. So the page also works out 80% of the tank's space at 130°F and uses the lower number. CHECK
    4. Stop at = TW + the most it should hold.
