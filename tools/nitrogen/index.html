Good to know

✅ Same gauge, same spot. Read from the same port with the same gauge each time.

☀️ Sun lies. A gauge or lineset in full sun reads high. Shade or overcast reads low. The air temp is a best guess, not the pipe temp.

📘 Follow the maker. Test pressure and hold time come from the install manual. Don't go over the low-side rating on low-side parts.

🧮 The math. Pressure goes with absolute temperature. Expected = (start psig + 14.7) × (new °F + 459.67) ÷ (start °F + 459.67) − 14.7.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo, a free weather service. When you tap a GPS button, your rough location (about 1 km) goes to Open-Meteo to get the temperature. It is saved only on this phone. This tool helps you judge a test. It does not replace the manufacturer's test steps.
