Read the cylinder

The leak sensor in the unit (RDS)

  • It sits low near the indoor coil, because A2L vapor is heavier than air.
  • When it trips, expect the blower to run, the compressor to stop, and electric heat to drop out. Zone dampers open and shutoff valves close on multi-coil systems.
  • If a new system runs the blower nonstop or locks out the compressor, check the RDS first.

Field rules

✓ Do
  • Check the area with your detector before you open the system
  • Get air moving and kill ignition sources
  • Use A2L-rated recovery machine, vacuum pump and detector
  • Charge R-454B as liquid and weigh it in
  • Purge with nitrogen, and braze with nitrogen flowing
  • Haul cylinders upright, strapped, out of the cab
  • Fill out the system label before you leave
✕ Don't
  • Top off or convert R-410A with an A2L
  • Mix refrigerants in one recovery cylinder
  • Vent it because it's "low GWP"
  • Work near pilot lights, live heat strips or smoking
  • Stand in smoke if anything burns (it can make hydrogen fluoride)
